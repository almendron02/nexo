# Nexo Spanish Foundations

## Curriculum and dependency map v1.0

**Four stages. Sixteen modules. One complete path.**

The course begins at zero and ends with the learner combining description, present actions, plans, relationships between ideas, and past narration. Lessons introduce concepts in dependency order and repeatedly move them through `introduced -> practiced -> produced -> reviewed -> integrated`.

## Final assessment

The final is separate from the Stage IV checkpoint and integrates:

1. Reading and listening comprehension.
2. Grammar control and sentence building.
3. Unsupported recall.
4. Original writing.
5. Spoken production with an accessible written alternative.
6. A short story combining the course.

## Master dependency spine

```text
sounds
  -> noun phrases
  -> gender and agreement
  -> subject and identity
  -> ser
  -> estar and hay
  -> questions and time
  -> verb architecture
  -> regular and irregular present
  -> verb + infinitive patterns
  -> objects and pronouns
  -> gustar and reflexives
  -> negatives and connected clauses
  -> subjunctive
  -> completed past
  -> background and habitual past
  -> integrated narration
```

## Sequencing rules

- Do not require a concept before its introduction, except clearly marked usable chunks.
- Reuse familiar vocabulary when the grammar is new.
- Introduce vocabulary through useful contexts, not disconnected category lessons.
- Recognition begins learning; unsupported production is eventually required.
- A lesson may award initial evidence, but long-term retention requires delayed mixed retrieval.
- Stage checkpoints are soft gates: recommend review when needed while allowing the learner to continue.
